# Wi-Fi (IEEE 802.11) generations and security context

## Bounded generation context

The [Wi-Fi Alliance MAC/PHY overview](https://www.wi-fi.org/wi-fi-macphy), accessed 2026-10-24, names Wi-Fi 4 through Wi-Fi 7 generations and describes selected features: its Wi-Fi 4 and Wi-Fi 5 entries use the 802.11n and 802.11ac labels; its Wi-Fi 6 text says Wi-Fi in 6 GHz was introduced alongside 2.4/5 GHz, without distinguishing Wi-Fi 6E or saying all Wi-Fi 6 devices use 6 GHz; and its Wi-Fi 7 text describes 2.4, 5, and 6 GHz. These are Alliance product-generation descriptions, not a complete IEEE 802.11 standards mapping. The [ESP-IDF v5.2.3 Wi-Fi Driver guide for ESP32-C6](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/v5.2.3/esp32c6/api-guides/wifi.html#esp32-c6-wi-fi-feature-list) lists driver features for that SDK target only; it does not establish another chip's support or a firmware integration path.

## Security context

The Wi-Fi Alliance [Security overview](https://www.wi-fi.org/security), accessed 2026-10-24, describes WPA3 and Protected Management Frames (PMF), including PMF requirements for Wi-Fi CERTIFIED devices; it does not provide a WPA2 comparison. Separately, the ESP-IDF v5.2.3 [ESP32-C6 feature list](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-idf/en/v5.2.3/esp32c6/api-guides/wifi.html#esp32-c6-wi-fi-feature-list) lists WPA/WPA2/WPA3 families as SDK driver features for that target, not proof that a cited application configures, negotiates, or successfully uses those modes. The Bruce auth-mode converter maps enum tokens to UI strings, but those labels alone do not establish protocol semantics or supported security generations ([pinned source](https://github.com/brucedevices/firmware/blob/a59213f3ec6cd302fa32c2042b2700c9c24d991a/src/modules/wifi/ap_info.cpp#L12-L25), code-inspected in the bounded Issue #100 evidence record).

## Applicability and limits

- Wi-Fi Alliance descriptions are source-reported product/technology context from the linked, undated pages viewed 2026-10-24; no IEEE normative standard text was inspected here.
- ESP-IDF v5.2.3 claims above apply only to its ESP32-C6 target documentation, not the ESP32-S3, ESP32-C5 coprocessor, Cardputer, or a particular firmware build.
- The M5MonsterC5 statement is reported by the pinned README; actual hardware, firmware, integration, RF behavior, and compatibility were not independently verified.
- WPA2-versus-WPA3 protocol semantics, device negotiation, and operation on Cardputer hardware remain unknown from this evidence set.
