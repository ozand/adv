# Strategic Goals — adv Cardputer Project

## Vision

Help Cardputer owners turn a compact device into a platform for learning, experimentation, and useful personal tools—without requiring each person to rediscover the ecosystem or start every implementation from scratch.

## Strategic goals

1. **Make the Cardputer ecosystem understandable.** Build an accessible map of projects, firmware, applications, and development approaches so people can explain what exists and which problems it addresses.
2. **Help people choose a starting point.** Organize knowledge around user needs, enabling owners to find relevant projects and compare suitability instead of browsing an undifferentiated catalog.
3. **Increase the owner's ability to create.** Learn from verified open-source implementations and turn useful patterns into understanding that supports original Cardputer projects and programs.
4. **Reduce repeated research and uncertainty.** Accumulate durable knowledge so future decisions build on prior evidence, expose gaps, and distinguish verified facts from assumptions.
5. **Grow a useful public resource responsibly.** Share reviewed knowledge that helps other Cardputer users while protecting privacy and safety and distinguishing this project from upstream work.

## Desired long-term outcomes

- The owner can explain the purpose, target audience, and limitations of notable Cardputer projects.
- Given a task, the owner can discover plausible projects and implementation approaches to investigate.
- Technical explanations point to upstream documentation or specific source revisions, serving as learning material rather than unsupported summaries.
- New research adds to a coherent body of knowledge instead of becoming disconnected notes or repeated searches.
- Public contributions help other Cardputer users without publishing restricted material, source archives, or sensitive operational data.

## Strategic boundaries

This is an independent knowledge and learning effort around the Cardputer ecosystem. It does not aim to mirror upstream repositories, replace their documentation, claim ownership of upstream code, or operate the physical device as part of ordinary research. Project execution mechanics belong in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md); binding architecture and publication decisions belong in [docs/adr/INDEX.md](docs/adr/INDEX.md).
